ECG KISS: Overall business
E – End Goal
The end goal is your business’s north star. It provides direction, clarity, and
purpose. Defining it clearly helps avoid distractions and ensures your time
and resources are aligned with what truly matters.
C – Current Pain Points
Understanding your current pain points reveals your present reality. This
step grounds you in facts and highlights the core issues impacting growth
and performance.
G – GAP
The gap is the measurable distance between where you are and where you
aim to be. Identifying this gap allows you to build strategies, make informed
decisions, and structure plans that bridge this difference effectively.
K – Knowledge
Domain knowledge is essential to understand how your business operates
from end to end. To quickly gain high-level insights, apply techniques
like skimming—extracting key information rapidly—to get started with
confidence.
I – Implementation
Break down your goals into detailed implementation steps. This helps you
think deeply, prioritize actions, and understand what needs to be done to
move forward strategically.
S – Simulate
Use flow diagrams or visual workflows to simulate how your implementations
will lead to the end goal. This gives you a practical preview, identifies gaps
early, and ensures better planning—saving time and resources.

ECG KISS: Overall Business | 9

S – Solution
With a clear picture of your business landscape, finalize the most suitable
solution. At this stage, you’re ready with a focused, actionable plan to
execute your process efficiently.
Why ECG KISS Works in Overall Business Planning
Creating a comprehensive business plan goes beyond setting goals—
it demands deep domain knowledge, real-world experience, and a clear
understanding of your current business operations. Every decision and
action must align with your long-term vision to truly move the business
forward.
But business doesn’t exist in a vacuum.
You must continuously navigate dynamic external forces—market shifts,
customer behaviors, emerging technologies, economic fluctuations, and
political developments. These ever-changing variables directly influence
your strategies and outcomes.
That’s where the ECG KISS Framework comes in.
Just as a doctor uses an ECG to assess heart health and determine the
next steps, this framework helps you evaluate the health of your business,
identify gaps, and implement structured, actionable solutions.
ECG KISS provides clarity, builds structure, and empowers you to move
with focus and precision.
It transforms planning from an abstract exercise into a powerful diagnostic
and strategic tool—keeping your business agile, aligned, and future-ready.

Why SLR CAMERAS Works for Yearly Planning
Across the world, strategic planning is often anchored around the yearly
cycle. Whether it’s businesses aligning to a financial year or governments
setting annual budgets, the rhythm of annual planning shapes major
decisions. Even employee appraisals and organizational reviews are typically
done on a yearly basis.
The SLR CAMERAS framework is designed to bring structure, clarity, and
direction to this crucial annual planning process.

By visualizing the full scope of your plan, you gain a big-picture

MC BEERS: Quarterly Planning
M – Milestones
List all the milestones you need to achieve in this quarter. Always keep in
mind how these milestones contribute to your yearly goals and long-term
vision. Select three long-term milestones, and then define other quarterly
milestones that support them. Additionally, include any high-priority
milestones that are unique or critical to this specific quarter, even if they
aren’t directly tied to the long-term goals.
C – Categorize
Add categories to each milestone such as Sales, Marketing, Production, or
Distribution, Cost Saving etc based on your industry. This helps organize
your plan for better focus and tracking.
B – Breakdown into Multiple Tasks
Break down each milestone into smaller, actionable tasks. This enables an
effective execution plan with priorities clearly marked as High, Medium,
or Low. Ensure your task breakdown aligns with the three long-term
milestones, the supporting quarterly milestones, and any critical, quarterspecific milestones. This approach helps maintain clarity, focus, and
balanced progress across both strategic and immediate objectives.
E – Estimate Efforts
Once tasks are defined and prioritized, assign estimated time and effort
needed for implementation. This step is crucial for proper resource
allocation and scheduling.
E – Evaluate
Evaluate your performance in the previous quarter:
•
•
•
•

How did you perform as a founder?
How did your team contribute?
How did customers respond?
How did your finances perform?

MC BEERS: Quarterly Planning | 35

Use a percentage or rating scale to quantify progress and identify gaps.
R – Review
Review all key learnings, insights, and improvements from the previous
quarter. This reflection helps refine your future plans and avoid repeated
mistakes.
S – Simulate & Schedule
Simulate your plan using a flow diagram. This helps visualize the path
ahead, clarify assumptions, and optimize use of time and resources. Then
schedule tasks and milestones across the 3 months of the quarter, adjusting
timelines as needed.
Why MC BEERS Works for Quarterly Planning
In both business and life, real progress becomes visible when efforts
are focused consistently over a three-month period. That’s why most
organizations review sales targets, evaluate performance, and implement
strategic changes on a quarterly basis.
Even in personal life, meaningful transformation takes time. For instance,
building muscle through a high-protein diet or reducing weight through
regular exercise and nutrition typically takes a minimum of three months
of consistent effort.
Quarterly planning provides the ideal rhythm—it allows you to take
focused action, track measurable progress, make timely adjustments, and
build lasting momentum without becoming overwhelmed by long-term
objectives.
MC BEERS empowers you to:
•
•
•
•

Translate big goals into manageable milestones
Stay focused with clear task breakdowns
Adapt with data-driven evaluations
Maintain momentum through continuous improvement

mindset. When applied consistently, MC BEERS keeps your team aligned,

It helps you stay connected to your long-term vision—while making

Prioritize each task on a scale of High, Medium, and Low. Also, identify
which tasks have the highest impact in moving your business forward.

across available days and evaluate whether your resources are underutilized
or overutilized.

How your team performed,
How you performed as a founder.

R – Review

improvement. Apply percentages to understand how much of the plan was
completed and what adjustments are needed.

Why PC PEERS Works for Monthly Planning
In both business and life, each month brings unique changes, events, and
demands. Seasons shift, customer behavior evolves, and operational focus
areas change—whether it’s planting in agriculture, peak sales in retail, or
budgeting cycles in finance.
Monthly planning allows you to stay agile and responsive without losing
sight of your broader goals. For example, businesses process customer
payments, pay salaries, and manage targets on a monthly basis. Each month
becomes a checkpoint to reflect, realign, and take decisive action.
That’s where PC PEERS helps. It enables you to:
•
•
•
•
•

Identify the most relevant yearly and quarterly goals that need
attention this month
Categorize and prioritize tasks based on urgency and impact
Estimate time and resources accurately
Evaluate and review past performance for continuous improvement
Simulate and schedule the month with clarity

By applying PC PEERS consistently, you can bridge long-term strategies
with short-term actions—moving from vision to execution with precision.
This monthly rhythm ensures that you are not just reacting to what’s
happening, but proactively shaping your outcomes with clarity and
confidence.

Why PFA SAAS SME Works for Processes

ADMINS ER: Crisis | 159

Why ADMINS ER Works for Crisis
Crises can occur unexpectedly in business. Recessions can delay or cancel
projects. COVID-19, for instance, changed how many businesses operated.
Resource shortages may happen due to delayed payments. Technology
upgrades can trigger an immediate pivot due to changes in resource use.
Your entire team may quit. Key partners or vendors may stop working.
Sudden price hikes in raw materials can disrupt operations. War or changes
in global policies can also impact business unexpectedly.
Accidents in factories can have an impact ranging from medium to severe,
potentially affecting delivery to customers.
The primary reason for the ADMINS ER framework is to help businesses
stay prepared in times of crisis—by identifying, addressing, and resolving
issues systematically.
