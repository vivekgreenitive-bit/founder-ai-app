## 🚀 1. Introduction to Founder AI

Welcome to **Founder AI** — the world’s first **Closed-Loop Executive Operating System** for startup founders.

Unlike generic AI chatbots that provide static text advice, Founder AI acts as your **Fractional Chief Operating Officer (COO)**. It identifies your primary business bottleneck using 13 proprietary frameworks, breaks down strategic advice into concrete action items, tracks your execution outcomes over time, and continuously refines its future recommendations based on your real-world performance.

### Key Capabilities at a Glance:
- 🎯 **13 Proprietary Founder Frameworks**: Built-in specialized decision frameworks (ECG KISS, SLR CAMERAS, ADMINS ER, etc.).
- 💰 **Founder Bottleneck Tax Calculator**: Quantifies the exact dollar revenue lost per month due to micromanagement.
- ⚡ **Execution Velocity Grader**: Real-time 0–100 scorecard measuring your company’s decision-to-action cycle time.
- 🔄 **Closed-Loop Execution Engine**: Auto-extracts action items from diagnoses, tracks completion status, and feeds outcomes back into future AI reasoning.
- 💬 **Google Cloud 1:1 Advisory Chat**: Enterprise-encrypted direct consultation with full business context memory.
- 🔒 **Offline-First Data Privacy**: Powered by local Llama 3.2 AI reasoning — your sensitive P&L and customer data stays private on your device.

## 📋 3. Step-by-Step Feature Walkthrough

### 3.1. Onboarding & Profile Setup
When launching Founder AI for the first time:
1. **About Your Business**: Enter your Company Name, Industry Sector, Stage (Pre-Revenue, Seed, Growth, Scale-up), and your **#1 Primary Quarterly Goal** (*e.g., Reach $10k MRR*).
2. **Primary Focus Areas**: Select your operational focus (Revenue, Churn, Product/Ops, Execution).
3. **Business Context Attachment**: (Optional) Attach a CSV, PDF, or P&L financial document to increase AI diagnosis confidence to 89%+.
4. **Founder Email**: Provide your email to generate your secure, cryptographic **Founder Token (`usr_...`)** for GCP advisory chat.

---

### 3.2. Core Journey Workspaces

#### 1. Today (Executive Command Center)
Your default home screen. Designed for 1-second executive clarity:
- **Top Metric Cards**:
  - 💰 **Bottleneck Tax**: Shows your current monthly revenue leak (*e.g. $9,742/mo*).
  - ⚡ **Execution Velocity**: Shows your 0–100 operational speed score.
  - 🎯 **Open Actions**: Total pending action items requiring follow-up.
- **Proactive AI Follow-up**: Prompts you to update open action items from your last diagnosis.
- **Recent Diagnoses**: Quick access cards for past business diagnoses.

#### 2. Diagnose (AI Business Diagnosis)
Where strategic problem-solving happens:
1. **Focus Area Selector**: Click a focus pill (*All, Revenue & Sales, Customer & Churn, Product & Operations, Execution*) or choose one of 13 proprietary frameworks.
2. **Input Challenge**: Describe your business problem in plain text (*e.g. "We have 15 leads in our pipeline but deals take 45 days to close"*).
3. **Attach Context File**: (Optional) Upload financial CSV or document.
4. **Run Diagnosis**: Click `🚀 Run AI Diagnosis`.
5. **Export & Share**: Once analysis completes, click `📋 Copy` or `📄 Export PDF` to download a shareable executive report for co-founders, advisors, or investors.

#### 3. Actions (Execution Workspace)
All action items generated by AI diagnoses automatically populate here:
- **Tabs**: View items by status — **Pending**, **In Progress**, or **Completed**.
- **Update Status**: Click `Start Working` to mark an item *In Progress*.
- **Record Outcome**: Click `Record Outcome` when finished. Log what happened (*e.g. "Conversion increased by 12% after updating landing page headline"*).

#### 4. Outcomes (Performance Log)
A historical audit log of all completed action items and verified business metrics. Founder AI reads this log before running future diagnoses to learn what strategies worked for your specific business.

### 3.3. Diagnostic Tools

#### 1. Founder Bottleneck Tax Calculator
Quantifies how much money your startup is losing by having the founder handle low-impact tasks:
1. Enter your **Target Hourly Value** ($/hr, *default $150/hr*).
2. Enter **Hours spent per week on low-leverage tasks** (*default 15 hrs/wk*).
3. Enter your **Monthly Company Revenue**.
4. View your **Monthly & Annualized Revenue Leak ($)**.
5. Click `⚡ Generate AI Delegation Plan` to immediately route to `Diagnose` with an auto-filled prompt to recover your hours.

#### 2. Execution Velocity Grader
Grades your company’s execution speed on a 0–100 scale:
- Measures action item completion rate %, stale pending items (>48 hrs), and decision-to-result cycle time.
- View status badges (**High Velocity 🚀**, **Moderate Velocity ⚡**, **Execution Bottleneck ⚠️**).
- Gives actionable recommendations to clear stale tasks and accelerate momentum.

#### 3. 60-Second Business Bottleneck Diagnostic
A quick 5-question diagnostic covering the 5 core business domains:
1. *Delegation*: >15 hrs/wk on admin?
2. *Operations*: Work stalls if founder is away for 3 days?
3. *Sales*: Leads taking >30 days to close?
4. *Retention*: Customer churn >20% in first 90 days?
5. *Cashflow*: Runway < 6 months?

Click `🔍 Identify My #1 Bottleneck` to instantly identify your primary leak and auto-select the matching Founder Framework.

## 🔐 4. Data Privacy & Security FAQ

### Q1: Is my sensitive financial data uploaded to public servers?
**No.** Founder AI operates on an **Offline-First** model using local Llama 3.2 reasoning. Your CSV uploads, P&L files, and financial documents remain stored locally on your device in your private SQLite database (`~/.founder_ai`).

### Q2: Is GCP Advisory Chat private?
**Yes.** Under Google Cloud Enterprise terms, Vertex AI data is strictly confidential, encrypted with TLS 1.3 in-transit and AES-256 at-rest, and is **never** used to train public Google AI models.

### Q3: How do I export my data?
You can export any diagnosis at any time by clicking `📄 Export PDF` on the Diagnose screen.
